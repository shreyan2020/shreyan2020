## Research

I maintain [Aligned AI Stack](https://github.com/aligned-ai-stack), bringing together study software, analyses, and experimental tools around belief, reliance, and human oversight of AI.

My research examines how experience with AI carries across tasks and languages, how people update their beliefs and decide when to delegate, and how they assess AI assistance in consequential decisions. I also study how the identity of virtual interviewers shapes people’s experiences.

An ongoing exploratory direction concerns scientific evidence reconciliation: assembling findings around a question while preserving source passages, experimental context, and the assumptions needed to combine them.
